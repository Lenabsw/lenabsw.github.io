Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Wordy Duck allows users to create a username to identify themselves while playing the game. Usernames and associated game data may be stored on our servers to support game functionality, including online multiplayer features.

Users are not required to provide their real name or email address to use the game.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Wordy Duck uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, app interactions, and advertising-related information to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and support its services. The information collected may depend on the user's settings and how advertisements are configured.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies .

3. How We Use Information

Usernames and associated game data are used to identify players and provide game features, including online multiplayer functionality. Third-party advertising services may process information for the purposes described in their respective privacy policies.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Wordy Duck account and associated game data through our account deletion request page:

Request Account Deletion

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed manually. Applicable account data will be deleted in accordance with our deletion practices and applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with Wordy Duck. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
